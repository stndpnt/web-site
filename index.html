01 — What we do

Two ways to work with us, depending on the scale of the build.

Most projects we take on fall into one of two categories — a single custom stand for a brand, or a large group build that brings multiple exhibitors together under one structural and visual system. The conversation starts with which one fits.

— 01 / Custom Stand Construction

Custom Exhibition Stands

For brands and exhibitors that need a unique stand concept, a strong visual identity, and full support from the first sketch through to installation on the show floor. Every project is drawn from scratch and tailored to the floor plan, the brief, and the way your team needs to use the space.

  • Bespoke architecture & spatial concept
  • Brand integration, graphics & wayfinding
  • Technical drawings & structural engineering
  • Photoreal 3D walkthroughs before approval
  • AV, lighting & interactive integration

— 02 / Group Stand Construction

Group Stand Construction for Pavilions & Multi-Exhibitor Areas

Black Point is StandPoint’s enhanced shell scheme system for group stand construction, national pavilions, organiser-led exhibition zones and multi-exhibitor areas. It combines consistent stand design with centralised technical, logistical and exhibitor coordination.

  • Group stands, national pavilions & multi-exhibitor zones
  • Consistent stand design across all participating exhibitors

02 — How we work

From brief to opening day — one controlled workflow.

We manage design, technical development, production and build-up as one coordinated process — with clear approvals, build-ready documentation and on-site control before the exhibition opens.

01
Phase 01

Brief & scope

We define the stand format, goals, floor plan, deadlines and technical requirements before design starts.

02
Phase 02

Concept & engineering

Design ideas are translated into buildable structures, materials, graphics, lighting and technical drawings for approval.

03
Phase 03

Production & preparation

Approved elements move into production, pre-build checks, packing and logistics planning to reduce risks before move-in.

04
Phase 04

Build-up & handover

Our team coordinates installation on site, resolves practical issues during build-up and prepares the stand for opening day.

03 — How we deliver

Everything needed to bring a stand to the show floor.

Behind every project is an integrated delivery setup: concept design, technical development, production coordination, logistics, installation and on-site support.

— 01

Concept & Design

Architectural concept, spatial layout, and visual direction shaped around the brand brief and the floor plan you've been allocated.

— 02

Technical Development

Structural drawings, materials, finishes, AV and lighting specifications — engineered for the venue, the regulations, and the build window.

— 03

Production Coordination

Joinery, metalwork, print, and finishing managed across in-house and trusted partner facilities, with quality checks before anything ships.

— 04

Logistics

Freight planning, customs and carnet handling, and venue access timing — so every element arrives on the show floor when the schedule needs it.

— 05

Installation

On-site assembly led by an experienced build crew, with day-by-day coordination against the venue's move-in plan and your stand readiness deadline.

— 06

On-site Support

A dedicated contact during show days for adjustments, technical fixes, and dismantling — so your team can focus on visitors, not on the stand.
