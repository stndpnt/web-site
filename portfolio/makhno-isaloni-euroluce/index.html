Custom Exhibition Stand

Makhno

iSaloni — Euroluce

An atmospheric exhibition environment created around sculptural objects, lighting and texture rather than conventional corporate branding. A large overhead volume defines the central space, while suspended forms, low plinths, a deep-relief feature wall and a curved partition create an immersive gallery-like setting. The restrained earth-toned palette and low, warm lighting allow the exhibited pieces to become part of the architecture itself. A long communal table and informal seating add a functional layer without disrupting the installation-like character of the stand.
