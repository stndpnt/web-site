Presentation Area

A deliberately open Black Point configuration for exhibitors whose products need to occupy most of the stand floor. With minimal built-in elements, the modular framework provides branding, lighting and a clear stand boundary while leaving the central area free for product presentation. Wall graphics or repeated banners can be added without reducing usable floor space, and a small meeting point can be incorporated at the perimeter. This approach works particularly well for furniture, equipment and other physical products that need to be seen, tested or compared directly on the stand.
