01 — What we do

A single studio for design, fabrication, and on-site delivery.

We don't broker, sub-contract, or hand off mid-project. Our designers, engineers, and crew chiefs work in the same building — which is why our stands ship on schedule and stand straight on opening morning.

— 01
Custom design

Custom exhibition stand design

Architecturally-led stand concepts built around your brand, your product story, and the floor plan you've been given. Every stand is drawn from scratch, structurally engineered in-house, and pressure-tested in 3D before a single panel is cut.

  • Concept design & spatial strategy
  • Structural & load engineering
  • Photoreal 3D walkthroughs
  • Brand & wayfinding integration
— 02
Turnkey build

Turnkey production & build

A 4,200 m² production hall, in-house joinery, CNC, metalwork, paint, and large-format print. We pre-build every stand on our floor before it ships, so what arrives on the show floor is the third assembly — not the first.

  • CNC, metalwork, paint, glazing
  • Pre-build & client walkthroughs
  • AV, LED & rigging integration
  • Freight, customs & carnet
— 03
Modular systems

Modular & reusable systems

For brands running 6+ shows a year, we design proprietary modular kits that scale from 24 m² regional stands to 400 m² flagships. Reconfigurable hardware, swap-in graphics, indexed crates — built to last 80+ deployments.

  • Proprietary modular hardware
  • Indexed crating & asset tracking
  • Refresh cycles & graphics rotation
  • Climate-controlled storage

On-site project management

A named project lead from kick-off through tear-down, plus a crew chief on the floor from move-in to handover. We work directly with venue ops, health & safety, and your stand staff so you can focus on the show, not the build.

  • Single point of contact, end-to-end

02 — How we work

From brief to tear-down, four phases, one team.

Every project moves through the same four phases — sized to the build, but never skipped. Most clients hand us a floor plan and a deadline; we hand back a fully engineered stand, freight-ready, on time.

01
Phase 01

Brief & concept

Two-week sprint with your brand and product leads. We come back with two distinct spatial concepts, a 3D walkthrough, and a fixed-fee build estimate accurate to ±5%.

10–14 days · 2 concepts · fixed-fee quote
02
Phase 02

Engineering & sign-off

Selected concept goes into structural engineering, AV integration, and venue compliance. You sign off on materials, finishes, and the production schedule before we cut anything.

3–5 weeks · structural drawings · venue permits
03
Phase 03

Pre-build & ship

Full assembly on our production floor with a client walkthrough. Stand is then crated, indexed, and shipped to the venue with all documentation, freight, and customs handled.

2–4 weeks · pre-build walkthrough · freight & carnet
04
Phase 04

Show floor & handover

Crew on-site from move-in to tear-down. Daily check-ins with your stand lead, 24/7 on-call during the show, full debrief and post-show photography delivered within seven days.

Show duration · crew chief on-site · 7-day debrief
