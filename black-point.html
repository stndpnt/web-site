01 — The system

One System. A Distinct Space for Every Exhibitor.

Black Point is a flexible exhibition stand system that combines a standardised structural base with individual branding and tailored stand configurations. It can be adapted to a unified exhibition zone or an individual stand of almost any size.

  • Group exhibition zones
  • National pavilions
  • Collective stands
  • Individual exhibitor stands
  • Zoning of large exhibition areas
02 — Configurations

From Standard Modules to Tailored Layouts

Black Point can be implemented as a standardised build with repeatable modules or adapted to the specific layout of an exhibition zone.
